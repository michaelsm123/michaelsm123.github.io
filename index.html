Wrench to Rocket: Idle Tycoon

Start with one lift and a wrench. Fix cars, hire mechanics, and watch your little garage grow into a whole city of car businesses, all the way to a rocket-powered space station.

  • Tap anywhere to earn cash, then hire help and buy upgrades.
  • Your garage keeps earning while the app is closed.
  • A living pixel-art city with a day/night cycle, an 8-bit soundtrack and optional light graphics.
  • Free to play, with optional rewarded videos and optional in-app purchases.
