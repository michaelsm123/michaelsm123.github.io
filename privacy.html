Privacy Policy

Wrench to Rocket: Idle Tycoon · Developer: MM Games · Contact: therightswipe.app@gmail.com · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Wrench to Rocket: Idle Tycoon ("the app") does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress is saved on your device.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress, settings (sound, music, graphics) and your choices (such as the analytics choice) are saved locally on your device. You can remove them at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Analytics (optional)

If you choose Yes when the app asks, it uses Google Firebase Analytics to record anonymous usage events, such as which game tiers you reach, which items and store packs you open or buy, and how often rewarded ads are watched. These events contain item, tier and product identifiers and counts. They do not contain your name, email or any text you type. Firebase may also receive standard device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, app version, and an app-instance identifier). You can turn analytics off at any time in the Store screen. If you choose No, nothing is sent.

Google's privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Crash reports (optional, same choice as analytics)

If you choose Yes, the app also uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to send crash and error reports (the error message, technical details of where it happened, and standard device information such as device model and operating system version) so problems can be fixed. These reports contain no personal information. Turning analytics off in the Store screen also turns crash reports off.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to show ads and measure them. In the EU and UK you will be asked for consent first. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly show personalised ads to children.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The app does not receive or store your payment details. Google handles billing, receipts and refunds under its own terms.

Feedback

If you send feedback from inside the app, the message you write (and, if you leave the box ticked, basic device and game information such as app version, device model, screen size, language, your game tier and total earnings) is sent to the developer by email. It is used only to fix bugs and improve the game.

Notifications

With your permission, the app can show reminders (for example when your daily reward or a new tier is ready). You can turn these off in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can turn analytics off in the app, limit ad personalisation in Android settings, deny notifications, or delete everything by uninstalling. To ask about data we may hold, or to request deletion of feedback you sent, email therightswipe.app@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
